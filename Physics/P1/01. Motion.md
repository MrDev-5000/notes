**Distance**
 - how far you have travelled form A to B
 - Si unit: $m$

**Displacement**
 - distance travelled in a stated direction
 - Si unit: $m$

**Speed**
 - distance travelled per unit time
 - Si unit: $ms^{-1}$

**Velocity**
 - displacement per unit time
 - Si unit: $ms^{-1}$

**Instantaneous velocity**
 - velocity of an object at a specific instant

**Acceleration**
 - rate of change of velocity
 - Si unit: $ms^{-2}$

**Uniform Acceleration**
 - equal distance covered in equal interval of time

**Instantaneous Acceleration**
 - Acceleration of the object at a given instant of time

### Equations of motion
- $s=ut+\frac{1}{2}at^2$
- $s=vt-\frac{1}{2}at^2$
- $v=u+at$
- $v^2=u^2+2as$
- $s=\frac{(u+v)t}{2}$

> [!info] Where
s → displacement
u →  initial velocity
v → final velocity
a → acceleration 
t → time
