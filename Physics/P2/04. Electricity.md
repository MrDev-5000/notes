- **Charge:**
	- b
	- SI unit: Coulomb (C)
	- SI base unit: $As$

- **Current:** 
	- rate of flow of charge
	- Si unit: $A$ - (Amperes) 

- **Potential difference:**
	- Work done per unit charge moving between two points
	- SI unit: $V$ - (Volts)
	- SI base unit: $kgm^2s^{-3}A^{-1}$

- **Electro Motive Force (EMF)**:
	- maximum potential difference between two electrodes of the cell when no current is drawn from it
	
- **Resistance:**
	- opposition of flow of charge
	- SI unit: $\ohm$
	- SI base unit: $kgm^2s^{-3}A^{-2}$

- **resistivity:**
	- resistance of a material of unit length & unit cross-sectional area at constant temperature
	- SI unit: $\ohm m$
	- SI base unit: $kgm^3s^{-3}A^{-2}$ 

- **Charge Carrier Density:**
	- number of free electrons per unit volume
	- Symbol: $n$
	- Unit: $m^{-3}$
	
- **Drift Velocity:**
	- average velocity of a free electron in a conductor, when a potential difference is applied to it.
	
- **ohms law:**
	- current through a conductor is directly proportional to voltage across it, when temperature is constant
