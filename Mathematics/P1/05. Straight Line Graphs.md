

### Gradient
$$m = \frac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{x_{2}-x_{1}}$$
### Parallel & Perpendicular lines
- parallel lines have same gradient, $m_{1} = m_{2}$
- perpendicular line have gradient $m_{1}= \frac{1}{m_{2}}$
### Linear Equation
- written as $y = mx +c$
- general form: $ax + by +c = 0$
- formula: $y - y_{1} = m(x - x_{1})$
### Length
- length of line $d$ with endpoints $(x_{1}, y_{1})$ & $(x_{2}, y_{2})$ is:
 $$d = \sqrt{(x_{2}-x_{1})^2 + (y_{2}-y_{1})^2}$$

### Midpoint
- the midpoint of a line segment with endpoints $(x_{1}, y_{1})$ & $(x_{2}, y_{2})$ is: 
$$M = \left( \frac{{x_{1}+x_{2}}}{2} ,\frac{{y_{1}+y_{2}}}{2}\right)$$

>[!TIP]
>the perpendicular bisector of a line segment ***AB*** is the straight line that is perpendicular to ***AB*** and passes through the midpoint of ***AB***.
