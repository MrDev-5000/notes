## Cosine rule
$$a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc \cos A$$
$$\cos A = \frac{{b^2 + c^2 - a^2}}{2bc}$$

## Sine Rule
$$\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}$$

## Areas of triangles
$$Area = \frac{1}{2}ab \sin C$$

> [!WARNING] The Ambiguous Case
> When applying the sin rule given:
> - an angle
> - an opposite side
> check if two valid triangles exist
> $\sin \theta = \sin(180 \degree - \theta)$
