differentiation gives the gradient of a curve at a point
 $$\frac{d}{dx}(ax^n) = axn^{n-1}$$

## Rules
- the derivative of a constant is zero, $$\frac{d}{dx}(c)= 0$$
- $$\frac{d}{dx}(c\times f(x)) =c\times f'(x)$$
