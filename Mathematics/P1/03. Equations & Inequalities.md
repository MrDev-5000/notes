### Simultaneous equations on graphs
- a graph of linear equation and the graph of quadratic equation can either:
    - intersect twice
    - intersect once
    - not intersect 

- after substituting, you can use the discriminant of the resulting quadratic equation $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ to determine the number of points of intersection.
    - $b^2 - 4ac > 0$  has two real solutions
    - $b^2 - 4ac = 0$  has one real solution
    - $b^2 - 4ac < 0$  has no real solutions
