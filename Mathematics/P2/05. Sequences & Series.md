## Arithmetic Sequence
- $n$'th term formula is $$u_{n} = a + (n-1)d$$
- the sum of first $n$ terms of arithmetic series is given by the formula $$S_{n} = \frac{n}{2}(2a + (n-1)d)$$
- this formula can also be written as $$S_{n} = \frac{n}{2}(a +l)$$
-

> [!Info] 
> - $d$ is the common difference
> - $a$ is the first term
> - $l$ is the last term 

## Geometric Sequence
- $n$'th term formula is $$u_{n} = ar^{n-1}$$
- the sum of the first $n$ terms of a geometric series is given by the formula $$S_{n} = \frac{a(1-r^n)}{1-r}, \qquad (r \ne 1,\space r < 1)$$ or $$S_{n} = \frac{a(r^n-1)}{r-1}, \qquad (r \ne 1,\space r > 1)$$- sum to infinity is given by the formula $$S_{\infty} = \frac{a}{1-r}$$
>[!Caution]
>this formula can only be used for a convergent geometric series, i.e. when $|r| < 1$
>

> [!Info]
> - $a$ is the first term
> - $r$ is the common ratio


## Recurrence Relations
- a recurrence relation of the form $u_{n+1} = f(u_{n})$ defines each of a sequence as a function of the previous term
- a sequence is periodic if the terms repeat in a cycle. 
	- for a periodic sequence there is an integer $k$ such that $u_{n+k} = u_{n}$ 
	- the value $k$ is called the order of the sequence 
- a sequence is **increasing** if $U_{n+1} > u_{n}$
- a sequence is **decreasing** if $u_{n+1}<u_{n}$

## Sigma notation
- signify a sum
- written as $\sum$ 
	- limits are written on top and bottom to show which terms you are summing
- $$\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^n$$
