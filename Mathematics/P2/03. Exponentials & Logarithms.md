## Laws of logarithms
- $a^m = x$ can be written as $\log_{a}(x) = m$
- $\log_{a}(x) - \log_{a}(y) = \log_{a}\left( \frac{x}{y} \right)$
- $\log_{a}(x) - \log_{a}(y) = \log_{a}(xy)$
- $\log_{a}(x^k) = k \log_{a}(x)$
- $\log_{a}\left( \frac{1}{x} \right) = \log_{a}(x^{-1}) = - \log_{a}(x)$
- $\log_{a}(a) = 1$ , $(a > 0, a \ne 1)$
- $\log_{a}(1) = 0$, $(a > 0, a \ne 1)$
- $\log_{a}(x) \times \log_{b}(a) = \log_{b}(x)$
- $\log_{a}(x) = \frac{\log_{b}(x)}{\log_{b}(a)}$
- $\log_{a}(b) = \frac{1}{\log_{b}(a)}$
