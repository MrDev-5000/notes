## Midpoint & perpendicular bisectors
- the midpoint of a line segment with endpoints $(x_{1}, y_{1})$ & $(x_{2}, y_{2})$ is: $$M = \left( \frac{{x_{1} + x_{2}}}{2}, \frac{{y_{1} + y_{2}}}{2} \right)$$
>[!TIP]
>the perpendicular bisector of a line segment ***AB*** is the straight line that is perpendicular to ***AB*** and passes through the midpoint of ***AB***.

## Equation of a Circle
- the equation of circle with center $(a,\space b)$  and radius $r$ is $$(x-a)^2+(y-b)^2=r^2$$ this can be expanded and simplified to 
$$x^2 -2ax + a^2 + y^2 - 2by + b^2 = r^2$$ $$x^2 + y^2 - 2ax -2by + b^2 + a^2 - r^2 = 0$$ $$b^2 + a^2 - r^2 = c$$ $$x^2 + y^2 -2ax - 2by + c = 0$$
- this is written as $$x^2 + y^2 + 2fx + 2gy + c = 0$$ where 
	- $r = \sqrt {f^2 + g^2 - c}$
	- and center  $= (-f, \space -g)$
