## Midpoint & perpendicular bisectors
- the midpoint of a line segment with endpoints $(x_{1}, y_{1})$ & $(x_{2}, y_{2})$ is: $$\left( \frac{{x_{1} + x_{2}}}{2}, \frac{{y_{1} + y_{2}}}{2} \right)$$
>[!TIP]
>the perpendicular bisector of a line segment ***AB*** is the straight line that is perpendicular to ***AB*** and passes through the midpoint of ***AB***.
