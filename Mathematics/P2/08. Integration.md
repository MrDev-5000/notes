## Areas between two curves
The area bounded by two curves can be found 
$$\int_{a}^b[f(x) - g(x)]dx = \int_{a}^b f(x)dx - \int_{a}^b g(x)dx$$

## Areas under x-axis
- when the area bounded by a curve and the x-axis is below the x-axis, $\int ydx$ gives a negative answer

## Trapezium rule
$$\int_{a}^b ydx \approx \frac{1}{2}h(y_{0}+2(y_{1}+y_{2}+\dots+y_{n-1})+y_{n})$$ where $$h = \frac{{b - a}}{n}$$ and $$y_{i} = f(a+ih)$$
